Hi,

I'm Madani. I love mathematics, physics, and the humanities, in roughly that order.

17, première at Lycée Arago in Perpignan. Most of my time goes to pure mathematics (differential geometry, analysis), some of it to physics (read for pleasure, not for rigour), and the rest to literature, Greek mythology, and the occasional political essay. I grew up in the Clos-des-Roses in Compiègne, and I still think about that a lot. On the side I run a free math circle, Perpimaths.

§ 03 · Perpimaths

A free extrascolaire math circle for kids in Perpignan.

Association loi 1901, filed March 2026. No fees, ever. Weekend workshops built around problem-solving, logic games, puzzles, and the kind of math that rewards intuition over memorisation. The stuff I wish I'd had access to at 14. Peer tutoring, short stages during school holidays, outdoor math days. Anyone can join; curiosity is the only requirement.

€2,000 raised so far seeking sponsors

We've raised €2,000 to cover our first year of workshops. For the next step, sending students to regional and national competitions, building a small library of textbooks, and hosting guest lecturers, we're looking for sponsors. If your company, foundation, or local institution supports youth education, please get in touch.
