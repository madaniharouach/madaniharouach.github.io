Teaching

AMMOC

At the Arnold & Marsden Mathematical Olympiad Circle I teach about ten students per week online, mostly olympiad preparation. Seven of them qualified for the AIME this year. I'm also a student there, with a directed reading project on differential geometry with Yaashaa Golovanov, and courses in set theory, linear algebra, vector calculus, and analysis I.

Workshops & outreach

  • India workshop, 2025 — Invited in an international online workshop to teach olympiad algebra to roughly 50 students during 3 days.
  • Morocco, summer 2025 — taught proof-based combinatorics to underprivileged students in my father's home village. Two hours a week for four weeks. The aim was to make teach them combinatorial arguements as well as the beauty of problems that requires just a small amount of theory
