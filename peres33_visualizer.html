How to play
Click any ray endpoint to cycle: uncolored → 0 (cyan) → 1 (magenta) → uncolored.

A triple is satisfied when exactly one ray in it is colored 0 and the other two are 1.

Violated triples turn red. The Kochen–Specker theorem says: with the correct Peres-33 set, no full coloring satisfies all triples.
