Madani Harouach

Hi — I'm Madani. I'm 17, in première at Lycée Arago in Perpignan. Most of my time goes to pure mathematics (differential geometry, analysis), some of it to physics (read for pleasure, not for rigour), and the rest to literature, Greek mythology, and the occasional political essay. I grew up in the Clos-des-Roses in Compiègne, and I still think about that a lot.

On the side I run Perpimaths, a free math circle for collégiens and lycéens around Perpignan. Association loi 1901, no fees, ever.

Currently. Working with Prof. Vincent Robin on an alternative proof of the geometric lemma in the Conway–Kochen free-will theorem, via group actions on the cube. Reading O'Neill's Vector Calculus & Differential Geometry. Listening to Górecki's third on loop.

Recently. Admitted to Stanford SUMaC for summer 2026 on a $9,000 scholarship — featured in Oise Hebdo in April.

"What I cannot create, I do not understand." — Feynman, on a blackboard, and it's stuck with me.
